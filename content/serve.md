## Mission trip: Guatemala

A team travels to Guatemala every June. Here’s how you can support them:

- **Food baskets:** give toward food that will be distributed in the village.
- **Building supplies:** help buy materials for a house and stove.
- **Quilts:** make quilts to send with the team.
{{< /feature >}}

## Hustonville Elementary

Make a donation to purchase food for the Backpack Program. **$20 feeds a child for four weekends.** We also collect clothing for students. All donations can be dropped off at the church.

## Operation Christmas Child

It’s time to pack your shoeboxes! Some ideas:

- **School supplies:** chalkboards with chalk, pencils with a sharpener, crayons, markers, paper
- **Toys:** ball with pump, dolls, jump rope, Hot Wheels
- **Make-your-own crafts:** add your personal touch
- **Non-liquid hygiene items:** bar soap, toothbrush, comb
- **Clothing accessories:** tops, socks, hair bows, gloves
{{< /feature >}}
