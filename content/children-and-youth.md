## Wednesday nights

A meal at **6:00 pm**, then lessons from **6:30 to 7:30 pm** for PreK through high school.

Wednesday night activities follow the Lincoln County Schools calendar. If Lincoln County is out of school, we won’t meet that Wednesday.
{{< /feature >}}

## Sunday mornings

**Sunday School** meets at 9:00 am in the basement, with a light breakfast at 8:45.

**Children’s Church** meets in the basement during the worship service, and a **nursery** is available every Sunday.
