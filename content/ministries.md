## Children & Youth Discipleship Night

Join us every Wednesday for a meal at **6:00 pm**, followed by discipleship for children and youth from **6:30 to 7:30 pm**. We have classes for PreK through high school.

We follow Lincoln County’s school calendar. If Lincoln County is out of school, we won’t meet that night.
