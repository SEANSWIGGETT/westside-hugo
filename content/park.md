Westside Community Church committed to building a destination public park on the US Highway 127 corridor in the Hustonville/Moreland area to meet the needs of the community’s residents and families for recreation, outdoor play, fitness and social connection.

**The park is open from dawn to dusk**, and everyone is welcome.
