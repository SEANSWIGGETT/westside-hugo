First time?

What to expect

Quick answers to the questions people ask before their first Sunday.

What should I wear?

Whatever you’re comfortable in. Attire is casual: come as you are.

What is worship like?

Contemporary music led by a live band. Worship begins at 10:00 am, and the nursery and Children’s Church are available during the service.

What about my kids?

A nursery is available during worship, and Children’s Church meets in the basement during the service for Preschool through 5th grade during the school year. Sunday School at 9:00 am has a class for every age.

Can I take communion?

Yes. Communion is served the first Sunday of each month and is open to everyone, regardless of age or membership.

Is there anything before the service?

A light breakfast is available at 8:45 am, followed by Sunday School at 9:00 am.

Can I watch online?

Yes. Sunday worship streams on Facebook Live, and Tuesday Bible Study at 6:30 pm is also on Zoom (meeting ID {{ site.Params.zoomId }}).
